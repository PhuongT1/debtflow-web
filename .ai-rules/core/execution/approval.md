# Resolve Uncertain Permission or Impact

**Activate:** unresolved safety/permission/impact after targeted investigation, scope expansion, or explicit developer review requirement. **Scope:** only the dependent proposed change.

Stop edits to that target. Complete authorized independent investigation and prepare a concrete reviewable proposal first. Do not make the uncertain change just to produce a diff.

The approval request must include:

- Requested outcome and proposed files/contracts to change.
- Evidence: purpose, known consumers, expected behavior, and checks available.
- Exact uncertainty or scope expansion; affected functionality/layouts and why existing authorization is insufficient.
- Bounded options: narrower correction where feasible, or explicitly approve the described broader change/risk.

Name/link the rule causing the pause and quote its applicable restriction. Ask for the actual decision; do not ask generic permission for routine understood work. Missing access may require evidence/access rather than approval to guess. If the developer accepts a documented uncertainty, keep the remaining verification limit explicit and respect higher-priority safety constraints.

Wait for an explicit answer before dependent edits. Continue only independent authorized work. Approval is scoped, persists across turns, and must not be requested again unless evidence materially changes that scope or risk. Silence, elapsed time, tool success, or a plan shared without a response do not grant approval.

**Exit:** specific approval/clarification received, a narrower authorized approach established, or the affected action remains pending.
